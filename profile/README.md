## AI-assisted CV screening and everything before the interview

Skilltage helps European recruitment teams define role requirements, collect applications and inspect candidate-specific CV evidence. Recruiters decide who progresses and control candidate communication.

Skilltage Unscripted adds one focused, recruiter-written question that a candidate answers by video on their own time. The candidate selects one answer; transcript-grounded review material supports the recruiter’s review. It does not evaluate appearance, emotion, accent or personality.

1. Define and confirm role requirements.
2. Collect applications and review source-linked CV information, gaps and uncertainty.
3. Invite a focused video answer when useful.
4. Review the material and choose the next step yourself.
